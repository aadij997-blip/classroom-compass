## Colors

- **Website anchors**, verified from the live stylesheet on 2026-11-24: cream `#FBFEEB`, ink `#1A1F14`, muted `#5A6350`, lime `#93D12E`, red `#AB0507`, pale green `#E7F3C4`.
- **Product extensions** (forest, blush, terracotta, amber, neutral) come from the handoff's `brand/tokens.css`. They are proposals, not an official brand manual.
- Dark ink is used on lime. Lime is never used as small text on cream.

## Illustrations

All concept illustrations in `src/components/art.tsx` are **original code-native SVG drawings**: choice cards, baskets, cash trays, checklist, and speech bubbles. They were drawn in the rounded style of the illustrated Day 1 lesson. No artwork was cropped from the flattened PDF pages, and no mascot or editable source art is claimed. The workbook-style doodle strip is a faint, original SVG pattern used only in banners, never behind data.

## References used (not shipped)
